# Playlist and crate ideas

## Working model

- **Crates = reusable labels/pools.** Order does not matter and a track can belong to many crates.
- **Playlists = ordered journeys.** Use them for a rehearsed set, a timed event segment, or a deliberate energy arc.
- **Auto DJ = a queue.** Feed it a playlist for a known order or selected crates for controlled random filling.

## Starter crate vocabulary

- Energy: `E1 Warm`, `E2 Groove`, `E3 Lift`, `E4 Peak`, `E5 Finale`
- Function: `Opener`, `Bridge`, `Reset`, `Singalong`, `Closer`, `Emergency Floor`
- Time/context: `Arrival`, `Dinner`, `Golden Hour`, `Late Night`, `Afterparty`
- Sound: genre, decade, language, instrumental/vocal, clean/explicit
- Workflow: `INBOX`, `READY`, `GRID FIX`, `REQUESTS`, `NEW THIS WEEK`

Avoid encoding every dimension in one long playlist name. Put reusable
dimensions in crates, then create concise ordered playlists from them.

## Playlist template

```text
YYYY-MM-DD — Event/Practice — Segment — v01
01 opener / establish tempo
02–04 build familiarity
05 bridge in key or percussion
06–08 raise energy
09 reset or contrast
10 peak
11 controlled exit / handoff
```

For every planned transition, note: incoming cue, outgoing cue, phrase length,
BPM plan, key relationship, EQ/filter move, and fallback track.

## Per-track preparation standard

- Hot Cue 1: first clean downbeat and normal mix-in.
- Hot Cue 2: main vocal or musical entry for orientation.
- Hot Cue 3: drop, chorus, or peak for phrase reference/performance jumps.
- Hot Cue 4: clean outro or final exit phrase for a reliable warning or recovery.
- Keep these meanings consistent across the library. This matches the four-pad-per-deck Starlight layout and reduces touchscreen dependence.
- Verify BPM and beatgrid before enabling Quantize. Quantize aligns an action to the grid; it cannot repair a wrong grid or poor phrasing.

## Initial playlist ideas

- `Rig Test — 30 Minutes`: known, clean files covering slow/fast, quiet/loud, short/long, and bass-heavy material.
- `Touchscreen Practice`: tracks with clear 16/32-bar phrasing for practicing navigation and cues.
- `Auto DJ Safety`: consistently tagged, analyzed, broadly compatible songs with safe endings.
- `Requests Inbox`: temporary intake; review into real crates after the event.
- `Transition Lab`: pairs grouped by a technique such as echo-out, loop-out, bass swap, or harmonic blend.
- `Six-Track Foundations`: coherent 6-track set for cue, phrase, fade, loop, and tempo drills.
