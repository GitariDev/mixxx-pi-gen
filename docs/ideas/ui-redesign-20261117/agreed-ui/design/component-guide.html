Components & tuning

Every control on the screen has one of six roles, and indicators show state. The colour tells you the role before you read the label, so a button that takes something away never looks like one that does the job you came for. Colours are muted from today's lime and saturated fills; a lit button keeps a see-through tint of its colour with a heavier border, so it reads at arm's length in a dark room without a flat fill. Buttons have 3 px corners; chips, which you cannot press, are pills.

Sound Changes what you hear now. ▶, CUE, pad TRIGGER, SYNC, KEY, Q, LOOP, effect ON Outlined in its own colour at rest. Lit: a see-through tint of that colour with a heavier border, which means it is shaping the sound right now.
Caution Takes something away. You can do it again. EJECT, pad STOP, HIDE, CANCEL scan Amber on a dark amber tint. Fills amber only under your finger. EJECT is Caution, never Confirm green.
Danger Cannot be undone. CLEAR (n), delete, the close ✕ glyph Red, and always behind a confirm step. The close ✕ is the one exception: a red glyph on a grey square, so it reads as close without shouting. It never deletes anything.
Indicators Shows a state. You cannot press it. SYNC and MASTER on the deck row; IN USE, READY, EJECTING and SAFE TO REMOVE on the Media page; the scan % on the USB chip; the key and BPM readouts Pill-shaped chips or plain readouts. No press state, so they never look like buttons.

Deck 2 orange and Caution amber stay apart two ways. By hue: deck 2 is a red-leaning orange (#d6784a, hue 20°), Caution is a yellow-leaning amber (#dfa33a, hue 38°), CUE is yellow (#cbb551, hue 49°). By fill: deck 2 controls are neutral at rest and the deck colour is solid only in the "2" badge; Caution sits on its own dark amber tint even at rest, and is the only warm colour that goes solid, while pressed. LOAD 1 and LOAD 2 on the deck rows keep their deck colour; a lone LOAD in a sheet or the plug-in ping is Confirm.

Radius and type

  • Radius: buttons and fields 3 px (also sliders, the close button and popovers); 2 px on badges, ticks and the slider handle. Indicators that cannot be pressed (chips) are pills. Today is 6 px on buttons, 4 px on chips, 8 px on search, 10 px on cards and 12 px on sheets.
  • Ubuntu Condensed (--mx-font-ui): every label you tap or scan: buttons, tabs, chips, screen and popover titles.
  • Open Sans (--mx-font-text): words you read: track titles, artists, menu rows, hints.
  • Ubuntu Mono (--mx-font-num): numbers that change: time, BPM, pitch, key, CPU.
