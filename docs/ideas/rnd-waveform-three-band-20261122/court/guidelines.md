# Guidelines: score bands (weights add to 10)

Score each direction per criterion, 1–10, by the band whose observations fit. Quote what you see in the image or the numbers.

## K1 Band separation (weight 3)
- 1–3: the three bands cannot be told apart; colour or shape gives one blended reading per column.
- 4–6: one band (usually the bass) can be picked out in some passages; the other two merge or hide each other.
- 7–8: all three bands can be told apart in most passages, but one band hides another in some columns or passages.
- 9–10: in every passage, each band's level can be read on its own at a glance, without one hiding another.

## K2 Mixing cues at a glance (weight 3)
- 1–3: kicks, drops and breakdowns are hard to find at 128 px; beat positions are lost in noise.
- 4–6: drops and breakdowns show, but individual kicks or the start of the bass are hard to see.
- 7–8: drops, breakdowns and where the bass comes in are clear; kicks show on most tracks.
- 9–10: kick positions, the bass entry, drops and breakdowns are obvious on both a loud and a quiet master, at arm's length.

## K3 Size and loudness (J13/J15 intent) (weight 2)
- 1–3: a typical track draws under a third of its space, or loud tracks sit flat on the edge.
- 4–6: a typical track draws a third to a half of its space, or one band is cramped.
- 7–8: a typical track fills about half its space and the bass looks big; loud tracks stop short of the edge.
- 9–10: as 7–8, and quiet masters are still easy to read.

## K4 Fit with the MixPi look and the owner's taste (weight 1)
- 1–3: garish or saturated colours; looks like a different app.
- 4–6: muted colours but an unfamiliar or busy look.
- 7–8: muted token colours; looks like it belongs to the Decks page.
- 9–10: as 7–8, and a DJ coming from rekordbox or a CDJ would read it without learning anything.

## K5 Cost and risk (weight 1)
- 1–3: needs a new offscreen buffer, legacy GL, or a large patch; or over 3 × today's per-frame cost.
- 4–6: a patch of a few hundred lines or about 2–3 × today's per-frame cost.
- 7–8: a patch around 100–150 lines and under 1.5 × today's cost; or config with a side effect (the Loop panel changes too).
- 9–10: config or skin only, under 1.5 × today's cost, no side effects.
