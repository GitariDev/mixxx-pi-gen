[`JOURNAL.md`](JOURNAL.md): its top section is the current master overview and
its dated sections record daily changes as bullet points.

## How to maintain it

- Add every session to `JOURNAL.md` under `YYYY-MM-DD`, newest first.
- Keep journal entries short, factual, and bulleted. Link to a deeper page for detail.
- Update the master overview whenever hardware, software, the default workflow, or a major blocker changes.
- Mark guesses as `Unconfirmed`; do not silently turn ideas into facts.
- Put repeatable procedures in `skills/`, music planning in `music/`, and experiments in `ideas/`.
