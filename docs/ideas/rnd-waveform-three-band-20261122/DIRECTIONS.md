Branch `rnd/waveform-three-band-20261122`, from `feat/pi-performance-target` at 17a8688. Nothing here ships without the owner's choice.

## Brief (five lines)
1. Owner, Climb hub, 2026-11-22: "Not sure if we can do a 3 band color scheme that separates the Bass Mid's and Highs." "Put it on me": the owner judges and chooses.
2. It serves the DJ at the Decks page: the two main waveforms (708 × 128 px each, on the 800 × 480 touch screen, at arm's length).
3. Good means: at a glance you can tell the bass (kicks, bass lines, drops) from the mids and the highs, and still read beats, drops and breakdowns.
4. Hard limits: J13/J15's height (pregain × 0.80, no ReplayGain; a very loud track stops about 10 % short of the edge), 30 fps on the Pi 4 (V3D), the overview strips unchanged, the muted palette of `tokens.css`.

The card shows **4 visual frames per pixel column** at that moment, not 3 (J13's script assumes 3; the difference is small for its height statistics).
- Every image is labelled SIMULATION. Track names are masked ("Track A", "Track B"); the overview strips and the deck strip are the real screenshot.
- The three-band colours in A–D are the tokens: low `--mx-deck1` #5b95d4, mid `--mx-deck2` #d6784a, high `--mx-text` #e4e7ec. (Pioneered's own SignalLow/Mid/HighColor are #004ee4 / #c95a00 / #f6e9d3, shown in `30-stock-options-at-J15.png`.)
- Evidence: `deploy/rnd-waveform-three-band-20261122/` in the main checkout (untracked).

## What Mixxx draws today
`[Waveform] WaveformType 17`, `allshader::WaveformRendererRGB`: one outline per pixel column (filtered.all, left channel up, right down), one colour per column, mixed from low × red + mid × green + high × blue and scaled so its largest part is 1. Pioneered sets no SignalRGB colours, so the stock red/green/blue apply. The three bands are present in almost every column (on this library the three band levels add up to 1.20 × filtered.all in the median column: `40-heights-on-library.txt`, ratio row), so the mix is mostly orange, pink and white: the bands do not separate.

## Stock options looked at and dropped
- **RGB Stacked, all-shader (type 25; the Mixxx enum's comment says 26, but the value follows 24):** the Filtered renderer with the SignalRGB colours. Same geometry as A, so it is A's bet; with the stock red/green/blue it is garish, and with J15 it clips like A-stock.
- **Stacked, textured (type 24):** `stackedsignal.frag` stacks the bands from the axis outward (B's bet) but mixes 53 % of the combined colour into every pixel, so the bands wash out; it draws into a 4 × oversampled offscreen buffer (2832 × 512 per deck, ≈ 87 Mpx/s of a branchy fragment shader at 30 fps), uses legacy fixed-function GL (`glBegin`, `glMatrixMode`) on the card's GL 3.1 V3D driver, samples one visual frame per fragment (no max), and with J15 21 % of a typical track's columns sit at the edge.
- **HSV (type 21):** one hue from SignalLowColor; saturation and value carry the band mix. It cannot show three bands.
- **Colour note for every direction:** the token palette puts deck 1's blue on the bass and deck 2's orange on the mids, so deck 2's row is mostly blue beside an orange "2" badge. Pioneered's own colours (#004ee4 / #c95a00 / #f6e9d3) have the same pairing. The palette is the owner's call after the pick.
- **Recolouring today's RGB renderer** (skin only): the bands co-occur, so any three colours mix to pastel; blue/orange/white mixes to lavender and white (`31-dropped-rgb-recolour-palettes.png`). Today's red/green/blue is already the most separating palette for this renderer.
